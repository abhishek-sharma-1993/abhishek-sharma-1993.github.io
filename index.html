About

  • Now: Staff Software Engineer, Visa Secure
  • Leading: a team of 4 engineers
  • Before: Porter, Jio Platforms, FirstHive, eMart
  • Based in: Bengaluru, India

I work on the Directory Server for Visa Secure, the part of EMV 3-D Secure that decides how a card payment gets authenticated before it is authorized. It's a system where a few minutes of downtime means real people can't pay, so most of my time goes into reliability, performance and making releases boring.

Before Visa, I scaled Porter's core services to handle 10x load, built fulfilment microservices and a Kafka retry library for Ajio at Jio Platforms, and ran a campaign engine and loyalty platform handling millions of requests at FirstHive.

I enjoy mentoring engineers through design and code reviews, and I'm always happy to talk about distributed systems, payments or growing into a Staff role.
